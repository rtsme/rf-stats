🤖 Full bot

Everything above, plus live announcements — kills, new chip bearers, boss respawn timers and achievement unlocks — and chip-war voice lines played into a voice channel. Several settings let you tune what it does.

Add the bot
After adding, run these in your server:
  1. /alerts channel #channel — where activity alerts go
  2. /alerts enable — pick what to announce (kills, chip bearer, bosses, achievements)
  3. /alerts channel #channel feed:Achievement unlocks — optional, sends achievements somewhere separate
  4. /soundalerts channel #voice then /soundalerts on — optional voice lines during chip wars
/alerts show displays the current setup. You'll need Manage Server to change these.
